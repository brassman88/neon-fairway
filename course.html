NEON FAIRWAY

  • You play with the three clubs you chose in Target Practice. Pick a club and a power %.
  • Click or drag on the course to aim.
  • The ball stops where it lands. No bounce, no roll.
  • Only the black fairway and the green are in play. Everything else is out of bounds (the bubbling purple ground).
  • The course has three sections of three holes. What happens when you land out of bounds changes in each section, and you get a short briefing before each one.
  • Balls that land on the green are putted for you.
  • First, a short training range shows how each kind of ground changes your shots.

Enter the 6-letter seed shown at the end of Target Practice.
